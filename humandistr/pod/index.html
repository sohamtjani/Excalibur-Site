04 / the pod

A small team with clear ownership at every step.

You are not handed from strategist to writer to production queue. The pod works from the same material, brief, and review notes.

01

Lead strategist

Runs the intake, sets the weekly priority, writes the brief, and owns the final decision on what is worth publishing.

02

Research lead

Builds the source bank from interviews, call notes, support questions, product material, and customer proof.

03

Writer and editor

Turns the brief into LinkedIn posts, scripts, customer stories, email drafts, landing-page copy, and edit-ready language.

04

Creator and producer

Turns approved ideas into carousels, short-form video, screenshots, diagrams, and channel-ready exports.

01

02

Brief queueApproved claims, audiences, proof, hooks, formats, and calls to action before production starts.

03

Production boardDrafts, edits, design files, video cuts, approvals, and final exports in one place.
