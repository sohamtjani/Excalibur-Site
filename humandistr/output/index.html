05 / the difference

A prompt is not a marketing team.

Most AI marketing models generate plausible drafts from thin context. You still have to supply the strategy, the domain knowledge, the editorial judgment, and the brand standards.

01

Generic AI starts from zero every timeA prompt does not know your product decisions, customer language, sales objections, competitive context, or proof. It gives you copy that sounds finished but still needs to be strategically rebuilt.

02

human.distr. gives you a complete podYou get a lead strategist, research lead, writer and editor, and creator and producer working together. Each role brings a clear responsibility and domain-specific experience from strategy through publish.

03

The team works inside your actual businessWe use product updates, founder sessions, customer calls, support questions, sales objections, customer examples, and live links as working material. That is how the work stays specific to your category and accurate to your offer.

04

The point is brand bettermentEach cycle makes the brand clearer. We sharpen what you sell, why it matters, how customers describe the problem, and which proof earns attention. You leave with stronger language, better evidence, and a team that knows what to do next.
