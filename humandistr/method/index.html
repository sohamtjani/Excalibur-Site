03 / the method

A five-part loop from raw material to repeatable signal.

The work starts inside the business, moves through one shared point of view, and returns as content people can recognize.

01

SourceCollect raw material from founder conversations, product demos, customer calls, support threads, sales notes, and work already in motion.

02

ExtractTag recurring language, objections, proof, customer questions, and moments that reveal what the business understands better than anyone else.

03

ChooseTurn the signal into two or three clear beliefs, a point of view, and a small set of repeatable content directions.

04

ProduceWrite, edit, design, and record from the same brief so strategy does not disappear during production.

05

LearnReview what people repeat, question, save, share, or act on. Feed that evidence into the next cycle.
