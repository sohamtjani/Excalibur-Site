03 / the workflow

From Monday intake to Friday publish.

The pod turns internal material into a brief, then into approved posts and assets your team can actually put to work.

01

IntakeSend product updates, call notes, sales objections, support threads, links, and anything the team is already discussing.

02

Source sessionTalk with the founder or subject expert. Clarify what changed, what matters, who needs to care, and what proof is available.

03

Content briefReceive a one-page brief with the audience, claim, supporting proof, opening hook, format, and call to action.

04

ProductionThe writer drafts the copy. The creator builds the visual or video. The editor checks accuracy, voice, and clarity against the brief.

05

Review and learnOne consolidated review pass produces final files. Response, objections, and useful language go into the next brief.
