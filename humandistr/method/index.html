03 / the method

Learn the business. Build the work. Repeat.

Your pod starts with the offer, customers, proof, and language, then turns them into a weekly cycle of strategy, content, distribution, and learning.

01

Map the businessYour offer, customers, sales motion, proof, competitors, and the language buyers actually use go into the pod's working brief.

02

Find the signalWe decide what deserves attention now, which belief needs to change, and what your business can credibly say that others cannot.

03

Build the briefEach idea gets an audience, claim, angle, hook, format, proof, and distribution plan before production starts.

04

Make and adaptThe pod writes, designs, films, edits, and reshapes the idea for the channels your customers actually use.

05

Ship, listen, repeatWe publish, track response, collect new questions and objections, and feed that language into the next brief.
