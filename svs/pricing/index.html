The four-week build

What the engagement is built to move.

The sequence is clear. The specific project is shaped around the student and the evidence that emerges along the way.

  1. WEEK 01

    Find the problem worth pursuing.

    Use skills, access, lived experience, and unfinished ideas to find a sharper starting point.

  2. WEEK 02

    Put the premise in front of people.

    Have real conversations, test the premise, and let feedback change the direction.

  3. WEEK 03

    Make the first version tangible.

    Build the pilot, public surface, pitch, outreach system, or first version the idea needs.

  4. WEEK 04

    Turn proof into the next move.

    Capture what happened and make a practical plan for what continues after the sprint.

What you are paying for

Work that makes the next decision easier.

SVS is designed to help a student leave the sprint with something more useful than a résumé line: a clearer point of view, evidence from the real world, and work they can continue to build on.
