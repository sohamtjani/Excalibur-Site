The real problem

The problem isn’t ambition. It’s disconnected effort.

Students are often encouraged to collect projects, titles, clubs, and experiences without developing a clear throughline. The result can look busy without making it clear what they care about, what they can do, or what work is worth continuing.

SVS helps students identify the work worth continuing and create evidence that makes their story credible to colleges, collaborators, and anyone considering what they bring to the table.
