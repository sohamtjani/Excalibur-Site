THE PARTNER PATHWAY

Most programs stop at the project. SVS keeps going.

Once a student has built relevant work and demonstrated exceptional results, SVS can connect that proof to startup founders and operators looking for people who can contribute.

A SELECTIVE PIPELINE

The project is the filter.

Students do not enter a partner conversation because they completed a course. They earn consideration by making good decisions, building something people respond to, and learning how to explain the value clearly.

Our direct pipeline is built around funded startups and founders who need people capable of taking ownership. That is what makes the pathway useful: partners see evidence of execution before they decide whether there is a fit.

HOW THE PATHWAY WORKS

Proof creates the introduction.

Every step gives the next conversation more substance.

01

Build evidence

Work 1:1 with MS/MBA mentors to find a meaningful problem, build the first version, and test it with real people.

02

Gain introductions

Capture traction, decisions, and results. Learn how to position the product and build a personal brand around the work.

03

Your Next Step

Students with exceptional ownership, execution, and fit may be introduced to a startup partner for a scoped role or conversation.

WHERE STUDENTS CAN CONTRIBUTE

Real work. Growth or technical.

GROWTH

Make the right people care.

Market research, positioning, content, partnerships, community, outbound, and the systems that help an early company find momentum.

TECHNICAL

Make the product work.

Product engineering, prototypes, automation, data, QA, and technical execution for students who can ship with care.

AFTER THE INTRODUCTION

A pathway, not a promise.

01Selective introduction

We share the student’s work and the reason there may be a fit.

02Scoped contribution

The founder and student clarify the problem, expectations, and next step.

03Performance becomes the signal

Continuation, paid work, or a future opportunity depends on the quality of the contribution.

Opportunities depend on student performance, role fit, partner availability, and the requirements of each startup. Paid opportunities are not guaranteed.
