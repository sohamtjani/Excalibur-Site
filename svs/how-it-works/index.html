The four-week system

Build the work.
Let the proof compound.

This is not a luck-based search for the perfect idea. It is a repeatable system for reducing uncertainty, finding evidence, and making better decisions.

One week at a time

From a hunch to a real next move.

What you leave with

Work you can explain, evidence you can use.

You should be able to answer: What did you build, who is it for, what did you learn, and what are you doing next?
